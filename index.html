About Us

With over three decades of experience in transforming spaces across Kenya

Your Trusted Gardening Partner Since 1990

Gathua Flowers and Gardening Services has been a premier provider of professional gardening and landscaping services in Nairobi for over 30 years. Founded in 1990, we have grown from a small nursery to one of Nairobi's most respected gardening services companies.

Our mission is to transform outdoor and indoor spaces into beautiful, functional areas that enhance quality of life and reflect our clients' vision. We take pride in our attention to detail, commitment to sustainability, and exceptional customer service.

Our team consists of highly skilled horticulturists, landscape designers, and gardening experts who are passionate about plants and dedicated to delivering exceptional results for every client.
